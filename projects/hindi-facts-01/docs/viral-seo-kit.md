## 5. Upload optimisation: settings that matter

| Setting | Use this | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Upload window** | 7:30–9:00 PM IST (Tue–Sun), or 7:30–8:30 AM IST for the office-commute window | Hindi daytime audience; Shorts push peaks 60–90 min after publish |
| **Category** | Education | Facts content earns Education browse + ad rates better than People & Blogs |
| **Audience** | **No, it's not made for kids** | Real death statistics — not child-directed |
| **Altered / synthetic content disclosure** | **Yes — "Synthetic / AI-generated"** | Voice is TTS, visuals are rendered (not filmed). Disclose; it does not reduce reach, and non-disclosure risks removal |
| **Shorts remixing** | Allow **video** remixing, allow **audio** remixing | Remixes are the cheapest distribution a factual Short can get |
| **Related video** | Link a longer India-facts video (or your playlist "भारत के रोचक तथ्य") | Keeps session alive after the loop |
| **Comments** | On, with "Hold potentially inappropriate" | The pinned poll drives the first-hour signal |
| **Language** | Video language **Hindi**, title/description **Hindi** | Triggers the Hindi Shorts surface; English captions would split the audience |
| **Captions** | Upload `hindi-captions.srt` (in this folder) | Burned captions cover sound-off, but the track feeds search + accessibility |

### First 60 minutes launch checklist

- [ ] Publish at the window above, then **stay in the comments for 15 minutes** (reply to every early comment with a question back).
- [ ] Pin the poll comment immediately after publishing.
- [ ] Share to WhatsApp/Telegram as a **WhatsApp Channel post** (not a status) — link previews need the title + cover.
- [ ] Post the cover image as a Community post 2 hours **later** (not before — Community posts can cannibalise the Shorts impression burst).
- [ ] Do **not** edit the title/description in the first 3 hours; edits reset the "new" boost.
- [ ] Check retention at the 1-hour mark: if average view duration < 60%, the loop seam is the problem — cut to the number earlier in your next upload.
- [ ] Reply to the first 10 comments with a follow-up fact (each reply pings the commenter back into the video).

## 6. Why this thumbnail earns the click

**Cover used:** `cover-a-do-sach.jpg` (split frame) — **alt:** `cover-b-19-only.jpg`

- **One idea, two facts.** Top half: Mars + a lander + ₹450 करोड़. Bottom half: a night road + 19. The brain reads "accomplishment + something wrong" in under a second — that unresolved pair is the click.
- **Face-free contrast.** No stock smiling face; instead a hard red-on-black numeral and a glowing road vanish-point. At 120 px wide in the Shorts feed the red 19 and the white headline are still legible (both above 30:1 contrast against the background).
- **Numbers do the work.** "19" and "₹450 crore" are pre-attentive — they read before any word does, and they are the two facts the video pays off.
- **Colour psychology.** Red = danger/urgency (the deaths), amber = India/pride (the achievements). The frame literally splits into those two emotions.
- **Motion implied, not shown.** Converging road lines + a lit lander suggest a moving shot; a static photo doesn't. Feed thumbnails that look "mid-motion" get more taps.
- **Text is 6 words max, all Devanagari** and centred in the top and middle thirds, so the Shorts UI on the right/bottom never covers it.
